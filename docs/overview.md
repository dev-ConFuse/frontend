# What is ConFuse?

ConFuse is a knowledge platform for teams. It brings your project's scattered
knowledge — source code, documentation, design notes, and links — into one
place, and lets you ask questions about it in plain language.

Instead of searching five different systems and reading twenty tabs, you ask a
question and get an answer that is **grounded in your own material**, with the
sources shown alongside it.

## The problem it solves

Knowledge about a project rarely lives in one place. It is spread across
repositories, wikis, design documents, chat threads and individual memories.
That creates three familiar problems:

- **Finding things is slow.** The answer exists somewhere, but nobody knows where.
- **New people take months to ramp up.** They cannot ask the questions they need
  answered without interrupting someone.
- **Answers are hard to trust.** A confident answer and a correct answer look
  identical, so people re-check everything by hand.

ConFuse is built around the idea that an answer is only useful if you can see
*where it came from*.

## What you get

| Capability | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Connected sources** | Bring in repositories, documents and web links. They become searchable together. |
| **Plain-language questions** | Ask the way you would ask a colleague. No query syntax required. |
| **Grounded answers** | Answers are built from your connected material, not from guesswork. |
| **Visible sources** | Every answer points back to the material it came from. |
| **Clear gaps** | When the sources do not cover something, the answer says so instead of inventing one. |
| **AI agent support** | Connect an AI assistant so it answers from your knowledge base rather than from general knowledge. |

## Who it is for

- **Team members** who need a reliable answer without a hunt.
- **New joiners** who are ramping up and asking the same questions everyone asks.
- **Tech leads and architects** who need decisions and rationale to stay findable.
- **Students and self-learners** who want to understand how question-answering
  over a body of knowledge actually works — see the
  [Learning Guide](/docs/learning-guide).

## What ConFuse is not

It helps to be clear about boundaries:

- It is **not** a replacement for your source control or document store. It
  reads from them; it does not take them over.
- It is **not** a guarantee of correctness. It shows you the evidence so you can
  judge quickly. For high-stakes decisions, read the cited source.
- It will **not** answer from its own general knowledge when your sources are
  silent. A clear "this is not covered" is more useful than a confident guess.
